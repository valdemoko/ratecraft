# Content Strategy — RateCraft

## Principles

1. **A page exists because a searcher needs it** — never to hold a keyword.
2. **Every guide answers: "why this page instead of Google or ChatGPT?"** — because it pairs
   with a live calculator, uses worked contractor numbers, and shows its method.
3. **No filler language** — no "in today's fast-paced world", no "whether you're a seasoned
   pro or just starting out". Direct, specific, edited prose.
4. **Formulas + worked examples + tables** where they add value; CTA to the calculator at the
   exact point of decision inside the body.

## Quality bar (checklist for every page)

- [ ] Original, specific, and correct — a trade publication would run it.
- [ ] At least one worked numeric example with real-ish job numbers.
- [ ] Formulas stated; assumptions and limits stated.
- [ ] Links to the primary tool (mid-body CTA) and 1–2 secondary tools.
- [ ] Links to 1–2 related guides.
- [ ] FAQ section answering PAA questions observed in research.
- [ ] Reviewed-date shown; pass through `/about` methodology standards.
- [ ] No clichés, no repetition, no padding. Read it aloud test.

## Published (Phase 1–3)

| How to Price a Job | how to price a job, how much to charge for a job | job pricing, labor burden, overhead calcs | ✅ |

## Pipeline (Phase 3 — build only if keyword-map priority holds)

1. How to Write an Estimate (what to include) — pairs with job pricing calc.
2. How to Calculate Labor Burden — pairs with labor burden calc.
3. General Contractor Markup: what's typical and why — pairs with markup calc.
4. Construction Profit Margins (benchmarks done honestly, sourced) — pairs with margin calc.
5. Job Profitability: checking a finished job against the estimate — pairs with margin calc.

Rule: a guide enters the pipeline only when (a) demand evidence exists in the map, (b) we can
add something the current SERP lacks, and (c) a tool pairing exists.

## Content types planned (later phases)

- **Glossary entries** (estimate vs quote vs bid; cost-plus vs fixed price) — Tier 3.
- **Trade pricing pages** (HVAC flat rate, plumbing, electrical) — Phase 4 with real tools.
- **Decision helpers** (hourly vs flat vs fixed-price) — tool+guide hybrid.
