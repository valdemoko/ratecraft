# Keyword Research — RateCraft

> Research date: September 14, 2026
>
> **Data honesty note:** Live search-volume and CPC figures require tools with licensed
> clickstream/Google data (Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner). This research was
> performed with web search and SERP analysis, so **volumes and CPCs are NOT invented** —
> where shown, they are marked as estimates with their basis. Difficulty/competition is
> assessed from observed SERP composition (who ranks, how strong their domains are, how good
> their pages are), which is a legitimate qualitative proxy.

## Methodology

1. Ran Google searches across the seed topics below (see searches log at bottom).
2. For each SERP, recorded: who ranks, what page type ranks, observed quality, PAA questions,
   related searches.
3. Classified intent (tool / informational / commercial) and derived opportunity ratings.
4. Volume bands use widely known industry patterns only where safe to claim; otherwise
   "n/a (needs Ahrefs/Semrush)".

## Explicitly rejected keywords (and why)

- **Roofing/painting/landscaping homeowner cost calculators** — homeowner purchase intent,
  dominated by strong brands (This Old House, homeguide). Wrong audience for a contractor
  pricing platform. Revisit only with a contractor-facing angle and real data.
- **"Employee cost calculator"** head term — ADP/QuickBooks brand SERP, low chance near-term.
- **Hourly rate guides for specific trades** (e.g. "electrician hourly rate 2026") — SERPs are
  dominated by salary/aggregator sites with real data we cannot replicate honestly.
